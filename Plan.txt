Phase 2 — Market Participants (the FII / DII / Pro layer)
This is where your real question lives. NSE classifies F&O participants into four buckets and publishes their data:

Code	Meaning	Who they are
Client	Retail + HNI	Individuals, HUFs, small firms
DII	Domestic Institutional Investors	Indian MFs, insurance (LIC), banks, pension funds
FII / FPI	Foreign Portfolio Investors	Foreign funds, hedge funds, sovereign wealth
Pro	Proprietary traders	Broker's own desk trading own capital (Zerodha prop, etc.)
Why each matters

FIIs drive trend — large flows move NIFTY heavily; their index futures positioning is widely tracked.
DIIs are usually counter-cyclical providers of liquidity; SIP inflows give a steady bid.
Pros are often short-dated and tactical — their options OI shifts can signal hedging/positioning.
Clients are mostly net option-buyers; their positioning is often a contra-indicator.
Done when you can describe a "FII sells cash + Pros sell index futures" tape without looking it up.

Phase 3 — Where the data comes from, end-of-day (this is what you asked)
After market close, here is the exact sequence of releases:

3.1 Cash-market FII/DII (≈ 17:30–18:30 IST, provisional; final by next morning)
NSE: nseindia.com → Reports → FII / DII Reports → "FII / DII Trading Activity" — provisional net buy/sell in cash market.
BSE: parallel page on bseindia.com.
Aggregator with history: moneycontrol.com → Markets → FII-DII Activity.
Telegram/X feeds repost the numbers within seconds — fine for speed, but verify against NSE.
3.2 F&O Participant-wise Open Interest (around 18:00–19:00 IST, for previous trading day)
This is the big one for derivatives traders.

NSE: nseindia.com → Reports → Derivatives → Participant Wise Open Interest (CSV).
Columns: Client / DII / FII / Pro × Long / Short × (Index Futures, Stock Futures, Index Call Options, Index Put Options, Stock Options).
Important nuance: this file is T+1 — Monday's data is published Tuesday evening. NSE explicitly delays it to prevent front-running.
3.3 F&O Participant-wise Volume (same page, next-day)
Useful to see who traded how much, not just net position.
3.4 Bulk & Block Deals (≈ 18:00 IST, same day)
nseindia.com → Reports → Equities → Bulk Deals / Block Deals.
Shows specific FII/MF names entering/exiting individual stocks.
3.5 SAST / Insider trading disclosures (rolling, end of day)
BSE/NSE corporate filings; tells you when promoters/insiders buy or sell.
3.6 Aggregators that pre-chew the data
Trendlyne — Participant OI dashboard with day-over-day deltas.
Sensibull / Opstra — option-chain analytics, OI buildup, PCR.
NSEpy / nsetools / jugaad-data (Python) — programmatic pull of all the above.
Done when you can pull yesterday's Participant OI CSV, compute net long/short per category, and spot if FIIs flipped from long to short on index futures.

Phase 4 — Reading the data (interpretation)
A standard end-of-day routine:

Cash market net flow — FII –1,200cr / DII +900cr → net institutional outflow ~300cr.
FII index futures net position — long/short ratio. A drop from 60% long to 40% long over a week is a positioning shift, not noise.
FII index options — call writing vs put writing tells you which side they're defending.
Pro positioning — pros often lead institutional flows by 1–2 sessions.
Client OI — usually opposite of Pros; widening gap = stronger signal.
PCR (Put-Call Ratio) of OI for nearest expiry — context, not a trigger.
Max Pain & key OI walls — heaviest CE/PE OI strikes act as magnets/resistance.
Build this as a daily checklist before you place a trade the next day.

Phase 5 — Derivatives Deep Dive (4–6 weeks)
Futures: contango/backwardation, basis, rollover %, cost of carry.
Options: intrinsic vs time value; IV; Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Rho).
Strategies layered by directional view: long call/put → spreads → straddles/strangles → iron condor/butterfly → calendars/diagonals.
Expiry mechanics: weekly NIFTY/BANKNIFTY/FINNIFTY days, settlement price, STT trap on ITM exercise.
Sources: Zerodha Varsity Modules 5 & 6 (Futures, Options). Sheldon Natenberg's Option Volatility & Pricing once you're comfortable.

Phase 6 — Advanced (ongoing)
Market microstructure: order book, market makers, HFT, latency arbitrage.
Volatility surface: skew, term structure, IV percentile/rank.
Algo / quant: backtesting frameworks (your bot is already here), risk-of-ruin, Kelly sizing, Sharpe/Sortino, walk-forward validation.
Macro overlay: USD/INR, US10Y, crude, SGX/GIFT NIFTY pre-open, FOMC / RBI MPC calendar.
