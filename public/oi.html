The Big Idea — Follow the Option Writers

Option buyers are usually retail. Option writers (sellers) are usually well-capitalised desks — they need margin, they hedge, and they don't sell options at a strike unless they believe price won't get there. So when writers aggressively sell puts below spot, they're saying "I'll defend this level" → Bullish. When they aggressively sell calls above spot, they're capping the upside → Bearish. No conviction either way → Neutral.

This bot is long-only and intraday. It only buys ATM CE on a confirmed bullish setup. Bearish signals are detected but not paper-traded.

Step 1 — Spotting a Real Writer

For each strike we look at the change in Open Interest (ΔOI) between snapshots and ask: is this fresh writing, or just noise / unwinding? A strike is flagged is_writing = true only when all three are true at once:

  • OI is rising (ΔOI > 0) — new positions are being added.
  • Premium (LTP) is flat or falling over the last ~12 min — sellers are pressing price down, not getting squeezed.
  • Volume ÷ OI < 0.6 — positions are sticking on the book, not flipping. High vol/OI = day-trader churn, which we reject.

Why all three? Because OI alone can rise from buyers or sellers. Only when OI rises while premium fades on low churn do we know the seller has the upper hand.

Step 2 — The 4-Rule Trigger (need ≥ 3 of 4)

For a BULL setup we scan every PE strike at or below spot inside ATM ± 5. For a BEAR setup we scan every CE strike at or above spot. Each candidate strike must score at least 3 out of 4:

  1. Unusual size — rel_oi_delta ≥ 1.6. This strike's OI build is at least 1.6× the historical median build for this time-of-day bucket. Filters out "normal" OI drift.
  2. Sticky positions — vol_oi_ratio < 0.6. Confirms the writer is committing capital, not scalping.
  3. Writing fingerprint — is_writing == true (the three-part check from Step 1).
  4. Whole-band agreement — the cumulative ΔOI trend across ATM ± 3 over the last 5 snapshots also leans the same way (4 of the last 5 diffs in the right direction).

If no strike clears 3/4, the engine prints no_trigger and we stand aside. That is the most common state — and it's the feature, not a bug. Most days will not produce a trade.

Step 3 — Building the Trade

If a BULL trigger fires and market is open:

  • Entry: Buy 1 lot ATM CE at latest LTP (× slippage factor).
  • Stop / Invalidation: the heaviest PE-writing strike at or below spot. If spot breaks that level, the writers' "floor" failed → exit.
  • Target: spot × 1.006 (about +0.6%).
  • Time stop: 15:00 IST — flat into close, no overnight risk.
  • Score decay: if the engine's score drops below 3 for 2 polls in a row while we're in the trade, exit early. The thesis has cooled.
  • Capital check: premium cost must fit current paper capital.
  • Throttle: one open trade at a time; capped per-day by maxTradesPerDay.

Why You'll See Many "No-Trade" Days

The Live Ticks table shows a Market Bias column ("Strong Bullish" etc.). That badge is a quick net-OI-flow z-score — a visual mood ring. It is not the same thing as the 4-rule trigger. You can have a "Strong Bullish" mood ring without a tradeable signal, because:

  • Unwinding ≠ writing. A rally driven by put-buyers closing their puts (negative Put ΔOI) looks bullish on PCR but fails rule 3 (is_writing) and rule 1 (rel_oi_delta goes negative). The bot deliberately ignores these.
  • Diffuse builds don't count. +50 L of put OI spread across 6 strikes won't clear the 1.6× bar on any single strike. The strategy wants concentrated conviction, not background drift.
  • Cold baseline. Until enough days of history accumulate, the baseline median is thin and the engine is conservative.
  • VIX shock. If India VIX swings ≥ 4% intraday, the whole day is skipped.
  • Outside window. Triggers before 09:45 or after 15:00 are ignored.

Rule of thumb: a quiet, sideways day with lots of unwinding will almost always be skipped. The bot is built to wait for one clean setup, not to participate in every wiggle.

Worked Example — A Clean Bull Trigger

Time 11:42 IST · Spot 23 715 · ATM 23 700.

Over the last few snapshots, 23 700 PE added +14.9 L OI while its premium dropped from ₹78 → ₹71, on vol/OI of 0.31. Baseline median for this 3-min bucket is 5.2 L, so rel_oi_delta = 14.9 / 5.2 ≈ 2.87. Cumulative ATM±3 trend: 4 of the last 5 diffs negative (CE − PE shrinking) → bullish.

Rule check on 23 700 PE: ① rel_oi_delta 2.87 ≥ 1.6 ✓ · ② vol/OI 0.31 < 0.6 ✓ · ③ is_writing ✓ · ④ cum_trend bullish ✓ — 4/4.

Engine emits BULL, score 4. Bot buys 1 lot of 23 700 CE at LTP+slippage. Stop = 23 650 (next heavy PE-write below). Target = 23 715 × 1.006 ≈ 23 857. Time stop = 15:00. Exit on whichever hits first.

Bearish mirror: same 4 checks on a CE strike at or above spot. Detected, logged, but not paper-traded (long-only).

Knobs (Where to Loosen if You Want More Trades)

Change one at a time and watch a week before changing another:

  • REL_OI_THRESHOLD in features.js — default 1.6. Most common cause of no_trigger. Lower to 1.3 → more signals, more false positives.
  • CUM_CONFIRM in features.js — default 4 of 5. Lower to 3 → trend flips faster, more chop.
  • ATM_BAND_SIGNAL in signalEngine.js — default ± 5. Widen to ± 7 → more candidate strikes, stops further away.
  • MAX_VIX_CHANGE_PCT — default 4%. Raise to trade more event days (riskier).
